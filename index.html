How it works

Wake up. Check the board.

1

Connect

Link the wearable you already wear: Apple Watch, WHOOP or Oura. Takes about a minute.

2

Score

Every morning, Splits scores your sleep and recovery against your own baseline, so a great night for you counts the same as a great night for anyone else.

3

Compete

Join invite-only groups with friends, teammates or classmates and see who's on top of the daily leaderboard.

Supported devices

WHOOP Any WHOOP strap
Oura Oura Ring
