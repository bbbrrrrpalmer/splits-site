Support

We're a small team and we read every email.

Questions, bugs, feedback, or need help with your account? Email us:

support@joinsplits.app

Helpful to include

  • Which wearable you use (Apple Watch, WHOOP or Oura)
  • Your phone model and app version
  • What happened, and a screenshot if you have one

Delete your account

Email support@joinsplits.app from the address on your account and ask us to delete your data. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Disconnect a wearable

You can revoke Splits' access at any time in Apple Health settings on your iPhone, or in your WHOOP or Oura account settings.
